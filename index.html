07 · Cross-cutting layer

Paid Media Menu

Paid media is social media, programmatic (DV360) and Google Ads — the three channels that sit outside any one title's own inventory. No pod owns this proactively today — every pod can attach it to a deal. Retail-media placements, newsletter takeovers, SEO-led content and video amplification aren't paid media — they're placements on a specific platform's own inventory, so they sit under that platform/title instead (Print, Digital Capacity, or the relevant campaign card in Monthly Plan).
